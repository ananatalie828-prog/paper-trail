Moving
A gentle, steady climb beside Avalanche Gorge through old-growth cedar and hemlock. Turning around at the foot of the lake makes it 4.6 mi — the extra 0.65 mi each way along the shore buys the classic head-of-lake view of the waterfalls.

Getting There & Back

1
Drive to the Avalanche Creek parking area on Going-to-the-Sun Road — about 16 mi past the West Entrance.
2
Hike out and back from the trailhead. No shuttle, no boat — the car stays put all day.
3
Drive back out the way you came.

On The Way

·
Trail of the Cedars — 0.9 mi accessible boardwalk loop sharing the trailhead. Worth the twenty minutes.
Parking fills before sunrise. The Avalanche lot is one of the first to fill on Going-to-the-Sun Road — be parked early, or ride the free park shuttle in and start from the Avalanche stop. Confirm whether a vehicle reservation is required for Going-to-the-Sun Road on your dates.
