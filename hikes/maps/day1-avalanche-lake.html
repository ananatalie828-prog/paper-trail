Moving
An easy grade the whole way, following Avalanche Gorge up through old cedar and hemlock. Most people stop at the foot of the lake, 2.3 mi in, which makes a 4.6 mi day. The shore path to the head of the lake adds 0.65 mi each way and puts you under the waterfalls.

Getting There & Back

1
Drive to the Avalanche Creek parking area on Going-to-the-Sun Road, about 16 mi past the West Entrance.
2
Hike out and back. The car stays at the trailhead all day.
3
Drive back the way you came.

On The Way

·
Trail of the Cedars is a 0.9 mi accessible boardwalk loop from the same trailhead. Takes about twenty minutes.
