The Boat Shortcut

~
Two short boat rides — across Swiftcurrent Lake, walk a few minutes, then across Lake Josephine — skip 1.7 mi each way. Take it out, back, or both. Ticketed and it sells out; book ahead, and note the last sailing back.
Bear country, and the trail is a ledge. Many Glacier is prime grizzly habitat — carry spray and make noise. The upper traverse is narrow with a long drop, and snow can linger across it well into summer; check trail status at the ranger station.
