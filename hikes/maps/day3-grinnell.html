The trail climbs the wall above Lake Josephine and ends at Upper Grinnell Lake, below the glacier. There are good views most of the way up, so anyone can stop early and head back without missing the point of it.

Getting There & Back

1
Drive to Many Glacier and park near the hotel and the Grinnell Glacier trailhead.
2
Hike out and back. Same trail both directions.
3
Drive home from the same lot.

The Boat Shortcut

~
Two boat rides, one across Swiftcurrent Lake and one across Lake Josephine, with a few minutes' walk between them. They save 1.7 mi each way and you can use them in either direction or both.
Many Glacier has a lot of grizzly activity, so carry spray and make noise. The upper section of trail is narrow with a long drop below it.
